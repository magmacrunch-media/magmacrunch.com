// privacy //

crunch//scope for iphone and ipad

CRUNCH//SCOPE collects no data. Your pictures stay on your device. Nothing you open, make or export is sent to magmacrunch media or to anyone else.

This policy covers CRUNCH//SCOPE, the magmacrunch media image instrument for iPhone and iPad. It does not cover the magmacrunch.com website.

no network connections

The app makes no network connections of its own. Everything it draws with, including its fonts, is inside the app, and it works the same with no internet connection at all. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no account to create.

your pictures

An image you open is read into the app to work on and is never uploaded anywhere. The app does not browse your photo library: you choose a picture in Apple's own photo picker, and only that picture is handed over.

What you make leaves the app only when you ask it to. EXPORT writes the finished image into the app's own temporary storage and hands it to the system share sheet, where you decide what happens to it: save it to Photos, put it in Files, send it to someone, or cancel. Saving it to Photos is the only reason the app ever asks for access to your photo library, and it asks at the moment you choose to save.

what stays on your device

The app remembers two things, both about how you like the instrument set up:

  • whether the grid over the display is on
  • whether the monitor is on, and which mode it is in

That is all. Chains, seeds and pictures are not saved between sessions. Nothing here identifies you, none of it leaves your device through the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

the clipboard

COPY puts a chain, as a short piece of text, on your clipboard so you can keep it or send it to somebody. PASTE reads a chain back. The app reads your clipboard only when you press PASTE, and what it looks for is one of its own chains.

children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does, and the date below will change with it.
