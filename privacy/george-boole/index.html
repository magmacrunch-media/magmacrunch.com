// privacy //

george boole for iphone and ipad

George Boole collects no data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to magmacrunch media or to anyone else, except your Game Center scores and achievements, if you choose to sign in to Game Center.

This policy covers George Boole, the magmacrunch media game on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. It does not cover the magmacrunch.com website.

no network connections

The app makes no network connections of its own. Its graphics, fonts, sounds and music are all inside the app, and it plays the same with no internet connection at all. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no account to create.

what stays on your device

To work properly, the app remembers a few things on your device:

  • your best score in each mode
  • the gates and discoveries you have unlocked in the gate codex
  • your settings, such as whether to show the math, and the mode you last played
  • which Game Center achievements it has already reported, so it does not announce one twice

None of this leaves your device through the app, and none of it identifies you. Like any app's data, it can be included in your device's own backups, which you control in your iPhone or iPad settings. Deleting the app deletes it.

game center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards and on your Game Center profile. Apple handles that under Apple's privacy policy, and magmacrunch media does not receive it. Game Center is optional: the whole game works the same without signing in.

links

The app's credits link to magmacrunch.com. Following one opens the website in your browser, outside the app.

children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does, and the date below will change with it.
