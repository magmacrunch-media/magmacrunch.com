HOW TO PLAY

Backgammon Rules

Backgammon is a classic two-player board game. The goal is to move all your checkers to your home board and then bear them off.

Setup

  • You are CYAN — your home is points 1-6 (bottom right)
  • AI is MAGENTA — its home is points 19-24 (top right)
  • You move from high points (24) to low points (1) — right to left on bottom row
  • AI moves from low points (1) to high points (24) — left to right on top row

Board Layout

13-18  |BAR|  19-24
12-7   |BAR|   6-1
  • MAGENTA's home: top-right (19-24)
  • CYAN's home: bottom-right (1-6)

Movement

  • CYAN: 24→13 (top), then 12→1 (bottom)
  • MAGENTA: 1→12 (bottom), then 13→24 (top)
  • Both move toward their home, then bear off

Gameplay

  • Roll dice to determine how many points you can move
  • Click a checker to select it, then click a destination
  • You must use both dice if possible
  • If you roll doubles, you get 4 moves instead of 2

Hitting

  • Landing on a single opponent checker (a "blot") sends it to the bar
  • Checkers on the bar must re-enter before other moves

Bearing Off

  • Once all your checkers are in your home board (points 1-6), you can bear off
  • Roll the exact number or higher to remove a checker

Doubling Cube

  • Before your turn, you can offer to double the stakes
  • If the opponent accepts, the cube value doubles
  • If they reject, they forfeit the game
