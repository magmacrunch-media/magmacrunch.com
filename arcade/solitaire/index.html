HOW TO PLAY

How to Play Solitaire

Click a card to select it, then click where to move it.

Build down in alternating colors on the tableau.

Build up by suit on the foundations (A → 2 → ... → K).

Click the stock pile to draw 3 cards. Only Kings on empty columns!

Scoring

+10 pts: Card to foundation  |  +5 pts: Reveal / waste→tableau

−15 pts: Foundation to tableau  |  −100 pts: Recycle deck

Win bonus: (700,000 ÷ seconds) × 35 — finish faster for a bigger bonus!

Klondike Solitaire

Original Game Concept

Klondike Solitaire’s exact origins are unknown, but the game has been popular since the late 1800s. This version is inspired in part by Microsoft Windows Solitaire (Wes Cherry, 1988; card art by Susan Kare).
