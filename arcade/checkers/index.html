HOW TO PLAY

Checkers Rules

Checkers is a classic two-player board game. The goal is to capture all of your opponent's pieces or block them from making any moves.

Setup

  • You are CYAN — your pieces start at the bottom
  • AI is MAGENTA — its pieces start at the top
  • Pieces can only move on dark squares
  • Regular pieces move diagonally forward only

Board Layout

  a b c d e f g h
8 . o . o . o . o  8
7 o . o . o . o .  7
6 . o . o . o . o  6
5 . . . . . . . .  5
4 . . . . . . . .  4
3 x . x . x . x .  3
2 . x . x . x . x  2
1 x . x . x . x .  1
  a b c d e f g h
  • o = AI pieces (top)
  • x = Your pieces (bottom)
  • . = Empty dark squares

Movement

  • Click a piece to select it — legal moves will highlight
  • Click a highlighted square to move there
  • Regular pieces move diagonally forward one square
  • Kings (promoted pieces) can move forward and backward

Capturing

  • Jump over an opponent's piece to capture it
  • Jumps are mandatory — you must jump if possible
  • Multiple jumps in one turn are allowed (and required!)
  • Click a piece that can jump, then click the destination

King Promotion

  • When a piece reaches the opposite end of the board, it becomes a King
  • Kings are marked with a crown symbol (♛)
  • Kings can move both forward and backward diagonally

Winning

  • Capture all of your opponent's pieces
  • Or block all of your opponent's legal moves
