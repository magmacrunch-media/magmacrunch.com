📖 HOW TO PLAY

GOAL

Be the first player to move all 4 of your pawns from Start to Home.

YOUR TURN

Draw a card, then move one of your pawns according to the card's instructions. If no legal move exists, your turn is automatically skipped.

ENTERING THE BOARD

A pawn can leave Start only on a 1, 2, or Sorry! card. It enters at your color's entry square.

CARDS
1Move a pawn out of Start, or move forward 1. 2Move out of Start or forward 2. 3Move forward 3. 4Move backward 4. 5Move forward 5. 7Move forward 7, or split the moves between two pawns (1–6 + remainder). 8Move forward 8. 10Move forward 10 or backward 1. 11Move forward 11, or swap places with any opponent pawn on the perimeter. 12Move forward 12. Sorry!Take a pawn from Start and place it on any opponent's square (bumping them home).
BUMPING

Landing on an opponent's pawn sends it back to Start. You cannot bump your own pawns.

SLIDES

Landing on the start of a colored slide (that isn't your color) sends your pawn zooming to the end — bumping anyone in between!

SAFE ZONE

After completing a full lap, your pawn may enter your color's Safe Zone. Pawns in the Safe Zone cannot be bumped. Move all 4 to the final Home square to win!
