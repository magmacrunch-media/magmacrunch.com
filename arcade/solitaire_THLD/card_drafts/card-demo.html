Design Notes

Face card concepts —

JACK (red suits) — Telecaster diagram. Notebook/diagram aesthetic from the album art. Diagrammatic, not illustrative.

JACK (black suits) — Birds on bare winter branches. "List of Birds by Flight Height" album track. Still/liminal.

QUEEN (black suits) — Moon over spruce. Direct album art reference. Dusk palette.

QUEEN (red suits) — Sunset highway + red lanterns. Highway/city duality from multiple tracks.

KING (all suits) — Band on stage. Pixel silhouette of the watercolor illustration. EXIT sign + "Delicious Organic Food" as easter eggs.

Number cards — instrument watermarks by suit (guitar ♥/♦, synth ♣, drums ♠ — or rotate as preferred). Opacity is 8% — felt rather than seen.
