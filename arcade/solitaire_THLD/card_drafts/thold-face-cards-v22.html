Design Notes — v22

① Queen figure — 5-point pixel crown, auburn flowing hair with curl at right, profile faces left, scepter with orb on left edge. Purple/violet gown with gold diamond pattern. Ruff collar matches Jacks.

② King figure — 3-arch crown (taller center), grey-white short hair, full grey beard with dark streaks (classic Bicycle motif), sword on left edge with gold crossguard. Navy + red diagonal sash on torso, same black/blue panel system as Jacks.

③ Pip zones unchanged — small corner index (K/Q + suit letter) outside inner border. Small corner pixel pip inside inner border TL/BR. Larger center pip in TL zone inside inner border, same approach as v16 resolution for Jacks.

④ Both figures use shared defs — queen-base and king-base are defined once and reused via <use> across all four suits. Suit color (black vs red) applied only to corner text, corner pip, and center pip — the figure itself is suit-agnostic.
