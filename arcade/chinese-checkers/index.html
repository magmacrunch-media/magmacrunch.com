HOW TO PLAY

Chinese Checkers Rules

Chinese Checkers is a classic board game for 2 players. The goal is to move all 10 of your marbles from your starting triangle to the opposite triangle.

Setup

  • You are CYAN — your pieces start at the top triangle
  • AI is MAGENTA — its pieces start at the bottom triangle
  • Each player has 10 marbles

Movement

  • Click a piece to select it — legal moves will highlight
  • Green highlights show adjacent moves
  • Yellow highlights show hop moves
  • Click a highlighted cell to move there

Hopping

  • You can hop over an adjacent marble into an empty space beyond
  • You can chain multiple hops in a single turn
  • Hops can go in any direction
  • You can also just move to an adjacent empty space

Winning

  • Move all 10 of your marbles to the opposite triangle
  • The first player to fill their goal triangle wins!
