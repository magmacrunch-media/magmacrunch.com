ABOUT
← back music videos network photography recordings releases works

A close relative to Vinny Bobarino, Juanito Thompson started as a home recording project during summer & winter breaks from college in 2009.

What began as improvised recordings in a spare bedroom evolved into a years-long collaboration with a rotating cast of musicians, all captured on cheap equipment in borrowed spaces.

The project has released ten albums across formats ranging from CDR home copies to streaming platforms, always operating outside any formal music industry structure.
