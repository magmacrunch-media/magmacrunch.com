"We are a Venn Diagram of brothers and physics grad school classmates from New Hampshire, New York and Wisconsin."

THLD was active from October 2015 to May 2016, playing four local shows in Iowa City. With neither the time nor budget for studio recording, material home-recorded during sessions in The Tuna Can was used to produce a series of albums around 2018–2019, originally released on Bandcamp.

The band's name — Texas Hold'Em Lava Dome — shares DNA with the cereal-mythology of Texas Toast Magma Crunch, one of their 2016 releases and the origin of the magmacrunch name itself.
