// The Tuna Can //
ABOUT

The Tuna Can was the de facto music space in the attic of a house on Evans Street in Iowa City.

After leaving Melrose House near Kinnick Stadium in 2015, Jake moved into this place on the east side of Iowa City. The house on Evans Street was closer to downtown and walking distance from the DC Buena, the venerable Iowa City punk venue that was open from 1994 to 2016.

The Tuna Can hosted two primary musical projects: Texas Hold'Em Lava Dome (THLD) and Audio Sound Paper (ASP). The two bands sometimes shared members, and occasionally played shows together at venues like the Yacht Club in Iowa City.

THLD was already in its "Texas Hold'Em Lava Dome" form by the time they started practicing at the Tuna Can. The band had formed from the ashes of weird things, a much earlier solo project by Jake that also involved Judah at Melrose House. Stephen had started hosting people at his apartment for music, eventually leading to THLD and ASP taking shape there before moving to the Tuna Can.

Audio Sound Paper — featuring Alex, Stephen, and later Dan — practiced separately from THLD but shared the space. The two projects had very different sounds: THLD was chaotic and loud, ASP was more structured and minimal. Both bands recorded at the Tuna Can in various forms.

The space was a classic Iowa City rental house attic: low ceilings, sloped floors, exposed wood, hot in the summer, cold in the winter. Not ideal for a music space, but it worked. The neighbors were generally tolerant.

The Tuna Can era lasted from 2015 to 2018, after which Jake moved to State College, Pennsylvania to live at Irvin House. THLD continued in various forms after the move, eventually recording Hot Dog at Sunnydale Sounds in 2022.
