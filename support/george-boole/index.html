george boole for iphone and ipad

George Boole is a sliding-tile puzzle about logic gates: slide numbers through XOR, OR, AND and NOT tiles and score from what they compute.

get in touch

For help, feedback or a bug report, email:

magmacrunchmedia@gmail.com

Bugs can also be reported on GitHub, where the game's source lives.

For a bug, it helps to include your device (for example, iPhone 16), its iOS version, and what you did just before it went wrong. A screenshot is worth a lot.

questions

how do i play?
Swipe to slide every tile. When a swipe pushes numbers together across a gate tile, the gate combines them, and you score for what comes out. Open settings and choose how to play for the rules, or turn on show the math to see every point worked out in binary as you play.
what is the gate codex?
A record of the gates you have used, with their truth tables and the discoveries you have made along the way. It fills in as you play; open it from the rules screen.
where did my best scores go?
Best scores and codex progress are kept on your device, so deleting the app deletes them. Scores already sent to Game Center stay there.
do i need game center?
No. The whole game works without it. To use leaderboards and achievements, sign in under Settings › Game Center on your device.
does it need the internet?
No. The app plays entirely offline.
can i play it somewhere else?
Yes, free in a browser at magmacrunch.com/arcade/george-boole.
