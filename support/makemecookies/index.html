makemecookies!x4 for iphone and ipad

makemecookies!x4 is a cookie factory you tend five stations of at once: flour into the hopper, dough out of the mixer, along the belt, golden out of the oven, boxed and shipped. A shift is one play of the song, and none of the five machines waits for you.

questions

how do i play?
Tap a station to act on it. The hopper takes a sack of flour, the mixer starts and then ejects a batch, the belt unsticks and speeds up, the oven gives you the tray, and packing ships the box. Each station wants you at a different moment, and that is the whole game.
can i lose?
No. There is no failure state and no game over, only how much you shipped and how much mess you left behind.
what are the stars?
How the shift went, counted in cookies shipped: one star at 8, two at 15, three at 22. They are set from what a very quick pair of hands can actually manage in one song, not from a round number.
what is RUSH?
Four windows in every shift where the belt speeds up and everything you ship scores double. The song is called x4 and gets four of them.
the health inspector turned up and everything stopped
That is the mess meter filling. The line freezes for a few seconds, and the song keeps playing, which is the penalty. Spills, burnt trays and oven fires all add mess.
the oven caught fire
Tap it three times quickly to put it out. Holding it will not work, and while it burns it is filling the mess meter.
where did my best shifts go?
Best shifts are kept on your device, so deleting the app deletes them. Scores already sent to Game Center stay there.
do i need game center?
No. The whole game works without it. To use the leaderboard and achievements, sign in under Settings › Game Center on your device.
does it need the internet?
No. The app plays entirely offline.
can i turn off the vibration?
Yes, with your device's own settings: Settings › Sounds & Haptics › System Haptics.
can i play it somewhere else?
Yes, free in a browser at magmacrunch.com/arcade/makemecookies.
