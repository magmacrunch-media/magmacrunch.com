crunch//scope for iphone and ipad

CRUNCH//SCOPE is a signal chain for images, played like an instrument. A picture goes in at the top, passes through the modules you patch together, and comes out at the bottom: pixel sorting, channel shifts, block corruption, Fourier filtering, feedback. You turn knobs to find things rather than typing numbers into a form.

get in touch

For help, feedback or a bug report, email:

info@magmacrunch.com

For a bug, it helps to include your device (for example, iPhone 16), its iOS version, and what you did just before it went wrong. If a particular chain does it, press COPY and paste that into the email: it is the whole chain in a line of text, and it reproduces exactly what you were looking at.

questions

how do i get a picture in?
UPLOAD IMAGE opens your photo picker. Or use one of the SOURCES, which generate a picture to work on instead: noise, gradients, colour bars, a checkerboard.
how do i get one out?
EXPORT writes the picture at its full working size and opens the share sheet, where you can save it to Photos, put it in Files, or send it to someone. The grid over the display is never in the saved file.
how do i change the order of the modules?
Drag a module by its name, or use the small arrows beside its switch. Order matters: the same modules in a different order are a different picture.
what are DICE and MUTATE?
DICE builds a whole chain from one number, so the same number always gives the same picture. MUTATE nudges the chain you have. UNDO steps back.
what is the screen under the picture?
The monitor. LEVELS is a histogram: how much of the picture sits at each brightness, with the source drawn behind in outline so you can see what the chain did, and a reading of the mean and how much is clipped at black and white. SPECTRUM shows how much detail there is at each size, and reads out the strongest repeating detail in pixels. MON switches it off if you would rather have the height.
what do PX and CYC mean on the knobs?
PX is a distance in pixels, measured at the 256 reference size, so a chain covers the same fraction of the picture whatever size you work at. CYC is cycles across the picture. Touch and hold a control to see what its unit means.
why is my picture small?
WORK SIZE is the size everything is processed at, and it starts at 256 by 256 so the knobs respond instantly. Raise it before you export; heavy effects take longer at larger sizes, and the Fourier ones take the longest.
some effects were rejected when i pressed DICE
That is deliberate. A generated chain that leaves the picture unchanged, or flattens it to mud, is thrown away and rolled again, so DICE gives you something worth looking at.
can i turn off the vibration?
Yes, with your device's own settings: Settings › Sounds & Haptics › System Haptics.
does it need the internet?
No. The app works entirely offline and makes no network connections at all.
can i use it somewhere else?
Yes, free in a browser at magmacrunch.com/ware/pixel-process.

privacy

The app collects no data and your pictures stay on your device. The full policy is on the privacy page.
