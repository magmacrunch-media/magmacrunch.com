what we're looking for

magmacrunch press is open to anything that doesn't follow the rules. essays, poetry, short fiction, experimental prose, science writing, cultural criticism, visual text, lists that become manifestos — if it's interesting, we want to read it.

we don't care about your credentials. we care about the work.

guidelines

  • no length restrictions — flash fiction to novellas, haiku to epic rants
  • simultaneous submissions are fine, just let us know
  • format: plain text, markdown, or PDF
  • previously published work is okay if you still hold the rights
  • multiple submissions accepted
  • we respond within 30 days (usually faster)

what we don't publish

  • AI-generated content
  • anything that exists solely to be inflammatory
  • content that harms or dehumanizes others

rights

you keep your rights. we ask for non-exclusive digital publication rights. if we publish your work, you can republish it anywhere — just credit magmacrunch press.
