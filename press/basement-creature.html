Basement Creature

april 2023

I'm not saying that I don't want you to think that you shouldn't do that. And I'm not saying I don't disagree with you.

But you didn't say you wouldn't concede to their terms. I've never known you to be someone who couldn't say no.

I'm not saying nothing is not fair game. And I can't say I haven't been there before.

Is is is?

Is is is.

What do you mean by is?

What do you mean by mean?

Mean means mean. But what is what?

What is what what is. And is is it.

And is is it??

Is is is.

I never thought you wouldn't deceive me.

I'm not thinking I couldn't never make it right.

I wouldn't think you didn't; wouldn't you?

I didn't think you wouldn't; wouldn't you?

I couldn't conceive that nothing wouldn't matter.

I don't think it wasn't nothing.

It ain't nothing.

Even nothing is something.

I couldn't possibly not care less.

I could, but do I should?

No.

It's not not a loft…

Tell me about it.

I shouldn't not but I won't.

So you won't, won't you?

I wouldn't, couldn't, but I shouldn't stop.

I can't say I wouldn't mean you wouldn't stop stopping.

I wouldn't be able to not laugh if you didn't.

I didn't not.

I won't not bite.

And I didn't promise to never punch you!

Good talk, I'll see you out there.

Yah, right!
